CONTROLS & KEY MAPPING

Click a box, then press any key or mouse button. Backspace clears it, Esc cancels.
Solo: the mouse moves the camera (click the game to capture it, Esc to release).
2-Player: Player 1 uses the left side of the keyboard, Player 2 the right side (or the number pad). Controllers are easiest: the 1st controller is Player 1, the 2nd is Player 2.
Controller: left stick move · right stick camera · A jump · X light · Y heavy · B special · RB block · LB grab · LT roll · RT transform · R3 (click right stick) lock-on · Start menu. In menus: stick/D-pad to move, A to pick, B to go back.

HOW TO PLAY

Win 2 rounds. Knock your rival's health to zero before the round timer runs out (change it in Settings).

Camera: move the mouse to look around freely. Press Lock-on (Tab / middle mouse) to keep the camera on your rival. A red arrow points to them when they're off screen.

Stances: press Stance to switch (costs a little energy). Each stance changes your moves and speed.

Auto-guard: a shield in front of you blocks small shots on its own. Blocked shots drain the glowing strip on your health bar. When it breaks, shots hurt until it refills. Light hits only stun every 5th hit, and after 2 seconds of non-stop stun you BREAK FREE.

Combos: light attack ×3 is a combo string, and the 3rd hit knocks them flying. If a light hit lands, press heavy to cancel into it.
Parry: tap block right before a hit lands. No damage, and a close-up attacker gets stunned. Mashing block won't work!
Heat: guns and ranged specials build heat (orange bar). At full heat your guns lock for 3s. Landing melee hits cools you down.
Tank: the hull drives where you steer, and the turret aims where you look.
Rivers: wading slows most titans (sea kaiju swim faster) and cools your heat.
Disasters: every city's disaster gets worse in 3 stages and attacks both titans: hurricane, eruption, blizzard, sandstorm, earthquake & meteors, and the flood.
Limbs: arms, legs and tails can be crippled. Turn on Realistic mode in Settings to have them come off.

Special attacks use the blue energy bar (fills when you fight). A hit from a beam blasts your rival back a whole city block!

Barrel roll: dodge with the roll button. You can't be hurt mid-roll, and it bounces shots back at whoever fired them.

Robots can grab cars, lamp posts and shipping containers. Swing them like a bat or throw them. Kaiju can't grab.

Watch your step: the tiny people will run and scream for help. Cars get crushed. Buildings fall.
