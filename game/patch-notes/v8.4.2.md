# Titan Clash v8.4.2 "Buildings Break Again" (Nov 7, 2026)

MajorasPride: "I can't destroy buildings... buildings need hitboxes too."

## Fixed
- VALOR's new kit (v8.3) and INFERNOTH's new kit (v8.4) only checked for the rival when a move landed, never for buildings. Swings went straight through them, and Destroy the City (no rival on the map) didn't work.
- Now every close-up move also hits the buildings in front of the titan, once per swing. Building damage: LIGHT 14, MEDIUM 28, HEAVY 55 (up to double when charged), SPECIAL 45, other moves 30. Buildings stay sturdy: a small building takes a few heavies.
- Ultimates, beams, shots, stomp shockwaves and grabs already had their own building damage and are unchanged.

## Files
- New: `tools/v8-src/39-building-hits.js`. `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.4.2`.

## Tested
Destroy the City, headless Chrome: VALOR's slash, bash and heavy chip and break a building; INFERNOTH's claw, tail whip and jaw grab take one down. No errors.
