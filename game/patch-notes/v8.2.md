# v8.2 "No More Stun Lock" (Nov 6, 2026)

Fixes the stun lock (worst bug) and adds the auto-guard. Planned with MajorasPride on Nov 6; nothing was removed.

## Fixed
- **Fire burn froze you.** Every burn tick restarted a short stun, so a fire hit froze you for 3 seconds, and the next fire hit started it over. Burn now only does damage. (ASHVIN and INFERNOTH start with Fire, so this happened a lot.)
- **Light attacks could loop forever.** Each light hit stunned for 0.3s and some lights are faster than that. Now light hits (light melee, and small shots that get through) always do damage but **only stun on every 5th hit in a row**. Hits 1-4 don't stop you moving or attacking.
- **Break free:** after **2 seconds of non-stop stun** (from anything), you're pushed out with "BREAK FREE!" and can't be stunned for 1 second.

## New: auto-guard
- Every titan has a **front shield that blocks small shots on its own**, no button needed.
- Blocked shots drain the **shield strip** (the glowing strip on top of the health bar) instead of health. Holding block while walking uses the same strip.
- Shield size comes from **Defense**: INFERNOTH 80, WORLDROOT 66, VALOR 66, RAGNAVOX 59, ASHVIN 52, RAIKOU 45.
- Empty = **GUARD BROKEN** (orange blinking strip): shots hit health (5th-hit stun rule). It refills 3 seconds after the last hit and comes back at half.
- Never blocks: melee, beams, specials, ultimates, or shots from the side/back.
- A timed parry still throws shots back. VALOR's Rally Stand and ASHVIN's Mind Barrier still work like before.
- Each titan's guard has its own look: VALOR blue shield flash, RAIKOU yellow sword slash, ASHVIN gold + silver circles, INFERNOTH heat wave, RAGNAVOX wing, WORLDROOT green.

## Changed
- The phone **⚡ FORM** button is now **⚡ STANCE**; the controls list says "Stance switch"; the How to Play page explains stances and the auto-guard.
- The old "STEADY!" rule for shots is switched off for the core six (the guard replaces it). The code is still there.
- Kept on purpose: the vehicle (transform) code. The six never use it, but the hidden Titan Lab still does.

## Code
