## Roster: 3 mechs vs 3 kaiju
Each country built its mech to counter one kaiju.

| Titan | Side | From | Built to counter / Countered by | Stances |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **VALOR** | Mech (leader, balanced) | United States | Counters INFERNOTH | SWORD / GUN |
| **ASHVIN** | Mech (psychic twins) | India | Counters RAGNAVOX | SUN TWIN / MOON TWIN |
| **RAIKOU** | Mech (lightning samurai) | Japan | Counters WORLDROOT | IAI / STORM / THUNDER GOD |
| **INFERNOTH** | Kaiju (fire brawler) | Natural disaster | Countered by VALOR | BRAWLER / MELTDOWN |
| **RAGNAVOX** | Kaiju (3-headed sound dragon) | Nightmare | Countered by ASHVIN | FIRE / STORM / ICE HEADS |
| **WORLDROOT** | Kaiju (elder world tree) | Alien seed | Countered by RAIKOU | WANDERER / ROOTED |

**Abilities (the ones MajorasPride picked):**
- **VALOR:** Shield Throw (bounces off the rival and buildings, comes back), Rally Stand (planted shield, small shots bounce back), Sword/Gun Swap Combo (swap stance right after a hit = STANCE BREAKER), Dawn Cannon (hold special to charge). Gun stance: rifle at range, shotgun blaster up close.
- **ASHVIN:** Telekinetic Throw (cars or a rock), Mind Barrier (shots freeze in the air, then fly back), Twin Split (a second twin fights for 6s), Mind Scramble (dazes + drains energy), Psy Bolts, Psy Burst.
- **RAIKOU:** Echo Shift (hold special: he stays still while 3 echoes run ahead; let go and he appears at the last echo and they all strike), three stances, Chain Lightning Blade (arcs through buildings back to the rival), Thunder Step (teleport behind the rival). In IAI stance, blocking counter-cuts.
- **INFERNOTH:** Sweeping Heat Beam (spines charge up, slow sweep), Dorsal Pulse (fire ring + burn), Magma Tail Slam (leaves lava), Burning Rage (lower health = hotter, stronger beam).
- **RAGNAVOX:** Sound is its main element: Roar Shockwave (cone, shatters windows, staggers) and Tremor Stomp. Element Breath uses the active head (fire burns, storm chains lightning, ice slows/freezes). FORM switches heads. A heavy hit can cut off the head in use (it takes damage first): that element is gone until it regrows (10s); at least one head always stays. Flight: jump again in the air (uses stamina); heavy while flying = Sky Dive.
- **WORLDROOT:** Future Portal Shot (a slow homing portal, not a beam: the rival sees the city's final-round disaster and takes weather damage), Root Whip (pulls the rival in), Overgrowth (grass that heals the tree and slows the rival), Time Skip (throws the rival to another part of the map), Root Eruption. Master of all elements: 4 element orbs circle its crown.

## Vault (kept safe)
All 12 old titans and the 5 extra maps are still in the game. **Settings > Vault** shows them again. Story mode still uses the classic cast until the new story is written. Launch map: **NEON CITY**.

## Fighting
- **Stances instead of transforming.** FORM switches stance in 0.3s for 8 energy (1.1s cooldown). Old vault titans still transform.
- **Light / medium / heavy.** Tap HEAVY = medium. Hold HEAVY = heavy (launches the rival into the air).
- **Energy like Budokai Tenkaichi 3.** Specials and ki blasts cost energy and have cooldowns. The Energy stat changes regen and costs.
- **Projectile blocking.** Blocking stops small shots completely (no chip, no stun). A timed block (parry) reflects them. Big projectiles are reduced. Shields and barriers reflect.
- **No more bullet stun-lock (all titans).** Small shots only flinch for a moment, and after 3 quick hits you're STEADY for 2s.
- **Quick escape (all titans).** The roll is now a short, fast dash in any direction.
- **ULTIMATE (Final Smash style).** Below 25% health, once per match, press SUPER. A cutscene freezes the fight, the camera moves to the titan, and it shouts a line in a cartoon font. **Who understands whom:** mechs and WORLDROOT speak plainly. INFERNOTH and RAGNAVOX only roar, unless you're playing a kaiju or ASHVIN (psychic twins) is in the fight on your side to translate. Titan Cores still give the old super.
- **Stats:** Health, Attack speed, Defense, Mobility, Energy (1–10) actually change the fight (move speed, damage taken, energy regen and costs).

## Build before the fight (Titan Files screen, page 3)
- **Focus:** Melee/Sword, Ranged/Mage, or Hybrid.
- **Element (one per titan for now):** Fire (burn), Water (slows, heals in rain/rivers), Lightning (chain zap, tiny stun), Steel (armor piercing + toughness), Anti-Magic (drains energy and breaks shields, but your own regen is 25% slower).
- **Ability tree (Diablo style):** class (Vanguard, Mystic, Striker, Colossus) → pick 1 of 2 sub-classes → 3 upgrades that unlock **during the fight** (start, after 120 damage or round 2, after 300 damage or round 3).

## Select screen
007-style **Titan Files**: manila folder tabs for each titan. Page 1 file (lore, country, counter, speech), page 2 stats with a radar chart and letter grades, page 3 abilities, ability tree and loadout.

## Cameras (Settings > Camera)
Titan Cam (default), free camera, **Over the shoulder** (Gears style), **Fixed cameras** around the city, **News Cam** from Chopper 7 (unlocks after your first win). **Scope:** hold C (or SCOPE on phone) to zoom, aim precisely and do +25% ranged damage (you move slower). Big titans: the Titan Cam pulls back and aims higher so a tall rival isn't hidden by the HUD, and steps to the side when your own titan blocks the view.

## Extras
- Settings > **Auto climb** toggle.
- **Citizens say funny things** at random ("IS THIS COVERED BY INSURANCE?").
- **Limbs:** bruised → crippled → missing, and each core titan regrows them its own way (VALOR gets an army air-drop, ASHVIN rebuilds them psychically, RAIKOU uses nanotech taken from a kaiju, INFERNOTH grows magma, RAGNAVOX regrows them, WORLDROOT grows new wood).
- Blade Shogun (vault) now swings its katana instead of punching with it.
- Cartoon font (Bangers, SIL Open Font License) is in `fonts/` so it works offline.
