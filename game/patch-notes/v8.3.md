# Titan Clash v8.3 "New VALOR" (Nov 7, 2026)

Everything MajorasPride approved in the sandbox moved into the real game, WORLDROOT came off the roster, and the Titan Lab code came out.

## What changed
- **Roster: 5 titans.** VALOR, ASHVIN, RAIKOU vs INFERNOTH, RAGNAVOX. WORLDROOT is archived in `archive/titan-clash-worldroot-archive.js` (his roster entry, body extras, moves and ultimate). A few harmless table entries keyed `worldroot` stay in the code; they do nothing while he's off the roster. RAIKOU is built to counter RAGNAVOX again (the three-headed dragon, as it always was), and his lore says so.
- **New VALOR** (Sandbox 1 to 12): Sword & Shield and Brawler (boxer) stances, each with its own stats, moves and ultimate. Rifle stance stays archived (`sandbox/archive/valor-rifle-archive.js`).
- **Combat for every titan:** LIGHT, MEDIUM and HEAVY are their own buttons (PC: J, K, L · 2P P1: F, G, H). Hold any of them to charge. Button order picks the combo string. Wall splat.
- **1 / 2 / 3 skills** with cooldowns and energy cost (keys 1 2 3, P2: 8 9 0, phone buttons with a spinning cooldown). VALOR and INFERNOTH have them for now.
- **Air stun-lock fix:** air light hits stun only every 5th hit, max 3 per jump, Combo Breaker after 10 hits.
- **Painted water** look on all of VALOR's moves.
- **Two ultimates:** TIDECREST OATH (sword) and SHIELD BARRAGE (brawler). Announcement card on press, a cutscene only if it lands. 1 in 3 play from a side-on fighting-game camera. Only the buildings in the way are hidden. The Chopper 7 box and news are hidden during cutscenes.
- **New fonts** (Press Start 2P, Permanent Marker, Rubik Dirt) in `fonts/ui-fonts.js`, licenses next to it.
- **Move list panel** (PC) rewritten for the new buttons, 1/2/3 and the stance's ultimate name.
- **Titan Lab:** its screen, code and the "+ BUILD YOUR OWN" card came out (`archive/titan-clash-titan-lab-archive.js`). Saved custom titans stay saved in the browser.

## Still the same
- The ultimate unlocks below 25% health, once per match (the sandbox's SUPERS READY test switch is off in the game).
- VOIDMAW stays sandbox-only until it's OK'd.

## Files
- New code: `tools/v8-src/10-valor-kit.js` to `31-moves-panel.js` (moved from `sandbox/src/`), plus `11-skill-keys.js` and `30-game-settings.js` (game settings for the code that came from the sandbox).
- `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.3`, adds `fonts/ui-fonts.js`.
- Sandbox: `sandbox/src/` now only holds sandbox-only code (test menu, VOIDMAW, fullscreen, speed). It's built from the game, so it has the same VALOR.

## Tested
PC 1366x768 and phone 844x390, VALOR vs CPU INFERNOTH: every button and string lands, 1/2/3 fire, both ultimates land and play their cutscenes, no errors. 2-player VALOR vs RAGNAVOX starts with no errors. The sandbox still opens with its full ⚙ menu.
