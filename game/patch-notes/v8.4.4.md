# Titan Clash v8.4.4 "Phone HUD on the Left" (Nov 7, 2026)

MajorasPride: "I don't like it sitting on the right, change it to the left for the rage bars. Buttons shouldn't be covered in general."

## Changed (phone landscape only)
- Your meter (INFERNOTH's ARMOR and RAGE, or VALOR's KO PUNCH bar) is one compact line on the left, under your health bar, right of the little titan icon.
- The rival INFERNOTH's ARMOR and RAGE are the line under it, marked RIVAL (they used to sit on the right, next to the buttons).
- Nothing covers the joystick or the buttons anymore (the old spot also overlapped the top of the joystick).
- PC is unchanged.

## Files
- New: `tools/v8-src/40-phone-hud.js`. `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.4.4`.

## Tested
Phone 844x390: VALOR vs INFERNOTH and INFERNOTH vs INFERNOTH, meters measured clear of the joystick and every button. No errors.
