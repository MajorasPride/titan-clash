# Titan Clash v8.4 "New INFERNOTH + Loadout" (Nov 7, 2026)

MajorasPride OK'd moving VALOR's and INFERNOTH's sandbox work into the real game. VOIDMAW stays in the sandbox. VALOR and INFERNOTH are still in the sandbox too (it's built from the game).

## What changed
- **New INFERNOTH** (Sandbox 13 to 13g): armor (light hits and small shots never stun him; 8 of them break it for 3s), rage with the tail-to-head glow, the BRAWLER kit (claw-claw-bite, tail whip, jaw grab and toss, seismic stomp, building smash / tail throw grab, Pounce, Burrow, Volcano Leap) and the MELTDOWN kit (lava spit, magma wall, eruption, heat beam with a lava trail, shove-off, Meteor Spit, Obsidian Shell, Volcano Core).
- **Three INFERNOTH ultimates:** KING (BRAWLER: jaw toss straight up, batter's wind-up, tail WHACK across the city), LAVA DOMAIN (MELTDOWN: one continuous high-heat beam, then 15s of lava rain), and the secret GROUND ZERO (ultimate ready + in rage). Every step has its own pose.
- **Loadout rework for VALOR and INFERNOTH** (Sandbox 14): two paths each (VALOR: SENTINEL / COMMANDER, INFERNOTH: MAGMA HEART / NUCLEAR BREATH), 3 upgrades that work in both stances, the 2nd and 3rd unlocked by goals (all 3 from round 2). Focus = move swaps instead of damage numbers. New element effects and matchups (Fire burns, Water slows, Lightning shocks every 5th hit) and full element looks. An upgrade card shows the path and the next goal.
- **Select screen:** PATH, FOCUS and ELEMENT each start on **DEFAULT** (the titan's normal setup). The titan file page follows the picked element (upgrade wording, what the element does and beats, the titan's look in it). Saved picks from before v8.4 start over on DEFAULT once.
- **Fixes:** Fury maxes out at +10% (was +30%). The CPU always gets a real element (it could get none in 2 of 5 fights). Water heals only standing in a river, not in rain.

## Still the same
- ASHVIN, RAIKOU and RAGNAVOX keep their old kits and old ability trees for now.
- VOIDMAW stays sandbox-only.

## Files
- New code: `tools/v8-src/32-infernoth-brawler.js` to `37-loadout-page.js` (moved from `sandbox/src/`).
- `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.4`.

Headless Chrome, PC 1366x768 and phone 844x390: VALOR vs CPU INFERNOTH and INFERNOTH vs CPU VALOR, every button, 1/2/3, both stance ultimates for each with cutscenes, no errors and no ghost buildings.
