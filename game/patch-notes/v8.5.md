# v8.5 (Nov 8, 2026): everything from the sandbox

MajorasPride: put everything we did into the main game; the sandbox has everything the game has, with switches to turn things off, and all new work starts in the sandbox. The game never has more than the sandbox.

Moved in from Sandbox 17 to 19 (code: `tools/v8-src/44` to `50`):
- **Fluid motion** (Soul Calibur style): springy joints, wind-up / snap / impact hold / ease-out on every move, a living fighting stance, walk sway, tails that drag, real hit reactions. `44-motion.js`
- **Lock-on flash**: the red marker and LOCK-ON tag show for 1 second, then fade; the lock stays on. **Roomier Neon City**: checkerboard layout, about a third of the buildings. `45-lock-flash.js`
- **MAGMA SURGE** replaces INFERNOTH's Magma Wall (MELTDOWN medium): a rolling lava wave that knocks down, eats small shots and leaves burning ground. The old wall code is in `archive/titan-clash-magma-wall-archive.js`. `46-magma-surge.js`
- **Camera views**: the 🎥 button (PC: C) flips TITAN CAM, ACTION CAM (over the right shoulder with a crosshair), OVER THE SHOULDER, FREE BEHIND, FIXED, NEWS CAM. `47-cam-views.js`
- **Flow**: shorter hit-pause that doesn't pile up, and no automatic Combo Breaker (the other anti-stun-lock rules stay). `48-flow.js`
- **New VALOR model**, Gundam model-kit style (inner frame + armor plates), new sword and kite shield. `49-valor-kit.js`
- **LIMB HITS**: claws, fists, feet, jaws, tails, VALOR's sword and ASHVIN's staff only hit when that part really sweeps through the rival during the move's active window. Close and mid range hit as before; the far edge of some moves is shorter (about 13 instead of 16). Ideas from Fray, Mugen3D and ThirdPersonSmashDemo (all MIT). `50-limb-hits.js`

The sandbox keeps on/off switches for all of these (⚙ → IN THE GAME SINCE v8.5) plus the Fight Lab test tools (hitbox view, frame data, move map). VOIDMAW stays sandbox-only.
