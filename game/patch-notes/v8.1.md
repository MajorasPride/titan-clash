# v8.1 "Clean Slate" (Nov 6, 2026)

Clearing out the old game so only the **core six** and **Neon City** are left. Everything removed is saved in MajorasPride's Google Drive. Nothing gets deleted: archives and every version's zip stay in Drive.

## Removed (saved in Google Drive)
- **The 12 old titans + THE RIFT KING** (Ironclad, Volt Rook, Blade Shogun, Magmaw, Riftclaw, Tidefang, Novawing, Wreckjaw, Gorynax, Kongamato, Komainu, Runewood): roster, lore, news stories, signature rules, their specials (Gorynax heads, Wreckjaw hook, Komainu twin spirit, Kongamato screech and sky carry, Runewood portal) and HUD lines.
  Saved in `titan-clash-old-roster-archive.js` (MajorasPride made it). A few small extra bits are at the end of `titan-clash-old-maps-archive.js`.
- **The 5 old maps** (Frozen Harbor, Desert Lab, Volcano Island, The Rift, Flood Zone) with their decorations, disasters (blizzard + whiteouts, sandstorm + tornadoes, eruption + lava bombs, meteors + quake, flood + waves), map hazards and their screen-weather lens effects (snow, sand, ash, Rift glitches).
  Saved in **`titan-clash-old-maps-archive.js`** (Google Drive, next to the roster archive). Each piece says where it lived in the code. The full old game is also in `titan-clash-v7.3.zip`.
- **The Vault** setting (nothing is left to show). An old saved Vault setting is cleared.
- **Steel and Anti-Magic** elements. Fire, Water and Lightning stay. New defaults: **VALOR = Water** (built to stop the fire kaiju), **ASHVIN = Fire**. Saved loadouts that used Steel or Anti-Magic switch to the new default.
- **The scope**: the C key (and P2's ' key), the phone SCOPE button, the crosshair, the zoom, the +25% ranged damage and the slow walk while aiming. Old saved key maps lose the scope key.
- **titan-clash-v6.html** (the old v6 backup file). The full old game is in `titan-clash-v7.3.zip`.

## Hidden until they're rebuilt for the core six
- **STORY MODE** shows "STORY · COMING SOON" (greyed out). The classic chapters used the old titans; they're in the roster archive. The story engine (TV special reports, chapters, ending) is kept for the new story.
- **TITAN LAB** shows "TITAN LAB · COMING SOON". It built custom titans on top of the old titans' stats and rules. Saved custom titans stay saved, they just don't show up for now.

## Changed
- **DESTROY THE CITY** is unlocked from the start (it used to unlock after story chapter 1, which can't be played right now).
- Kept on purpose: all the body builders (robot, samurai, volcano beast, crab, serpent, winged dragon, tree, lion), because the core six and the future Titan Lab use them. The katana-swing fix stays for the samurai body. The lava pools stay (INFERNOTH's Magma Tail leaves them). The time-portal screen effect stays (WORLDROOT's Future Portal uses it).
- Neon City keeps its own disaster (hurricane → city blackout) and its trigger (the power substation).

## Tested
- PC (1366×768) and phone (landscape 844×390, touch) in headless Chrome, opened as a file with no internet (local `three.min.js`).
- All six titans in CPU fights (with a CPU flying the player too), 2 player split screen, Destroy the City, the hurricane up to round 3, the select screen loadout page, Settings, Controls. Pressing C does nothing. No script errors.
- Old v8.0 saves (Steel/Anti-Magic loadouts, Vault on, a custom titan) load without errors.
