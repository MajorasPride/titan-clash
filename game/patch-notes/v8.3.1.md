# Titan Clash v8.3.1 bug fixes (Nov 7, 2026)

Two bugs MajorasPride found in Sandbox 14 that were also in the real game.

## Fixed
- **KO! stuck on the heavy button.** Playing VALOR's Brawler with a full KO meter turns the HEAVY button into KO!. Switching to another titan left it saying KO! for everyone. It now goes back to HEAVY. (`tools/v8-src/14-valor-boxer.js`)
- **Ghost buildings after ultimates.** Cutscenes hide the buildings between the camera and the titans. A building smashed while it was hidden came back as a ghost when the cutscene ended. Smashed buildings now stay gone; standing ones come back as before. (`tools/v8-src/24-cine-view.js`)
- Small change for the sandbox: SUPERS READY no longer fills VALOR's KO meter when the sandbox's METERS tool is in charge (`SB.meterCtl`). The game has SUPERS READY off, so nothing changes in play.

## Files
- `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.3.1`.

## Tested
Headless Chrome: a building hidden for the camera and then smashed stays gone after the cutscene; a standing one comes back. The heavy label goes back to HEAVY after switching from VALOR to INFERNOTH. No errors.
