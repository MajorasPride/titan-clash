# Titan Clash v8.4.5 "The Twins + Combo Feel" (Nov 7, 2026)

MajorasPride: the combos still felt weird ("all of it"), then "push it to the game". VOIDMAW stays sandbox-only.

## New ASHVIN (Sandbox 15 to 15g)
- Two twins as stances: **SUN** (he: fast, fiery, buffs himself, up close) and **MOON** (she: slower, harder hits, waves and curses, from range). One bow-staff held in the middle; Sun in his right hand, Moon in her left. Swapping twins is faster and cheaper.
- **Element pairs** on the select page: DEFAULT / FIRE + ICE / LIGHTNING + WATER. Ice is new and ASHVIN-only (slows). Water soaks; soaked rivals take +25% from Sun's lightning.
- **Tag-swap strings:** L L H or M H ends with the other twin cutting in (Moon: cold wave that shoves them to her range; Sun: a burst that pops them up close).
- **SUN:** staff combo, spinning strike, flame slam, sun rune (flame blast trap), Blaze Up, Flame Dash, Solar Flare (blinds, knocks off lock-on), pole-vault grab.
- **MOON:** heavy staff, frost wave, ice spike, curse rune (slow + damage over time), Hex, Frost Prison, Drain, ice birdcage grab.
- **Ultimates:** SOLAR MANDALA (rush in; air strikes draw a sun god symbol, slam, combined twin beam across the city; "THE SUN NEVER SETS ON US!" / "TWO HALVES, ONE SKY!") and TOTAL ECLIPSE (aimed; the world goes black under a black moon, ice pillar, Sun spikes them down; "NIGHT FALLS... FOR YOU." / "SLEEP UNDER OUR ECLIPSE.").
- Painted staff ribbons and hits in the fighting twin's element, in the same ink style as VALOR's water.

## Combo feel (every titan)
- A tiny hit-pause on every landed hit (bigger for heavies, string enders and charged hits) and a visible flinch on the rival. The flinch is only a look: it doesn't stun, so the v8.2 anti-stun-lock rules are unchanged.
- A whiffed light can be cancelled into the next button a little after its hit moment (it used to lock you into the whole swing).
