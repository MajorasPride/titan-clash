# Titan Clash v8.4.3 "Titan Core + HUD Fix" (Nov 7, 2026)

Two bugs MajorasPride found playing VALOR vs INFERNOTH on his phone.

## Fixed
- **The Titan Core didn't fire VALOR's ultimates.** Grabbing a Core and pressing SUPER ran the old generic super from before v8.3 (a short pose, no cutscene). It now fires the titan's real ultimate for the stance you're in (VALOR: TIDECREST OATH or SHIELD BARRAGE; INFERNOTH: KING or LAVA DOMAIN; the CPU too). Using a Core doesn't spend your once-per-match low-health ultimate. Pickup text now says "TITAN CORE! ULTIMATE READY!".
- **HUD pileup on phone.** VALOR's KO Punch bar wrapped onto three lines on top of the combo counter and the Fury text. It's now one line under the stance label. The rival INFERNOTH's armor/rage bars sat on the touch buttons; they're now under the rival's health bar, left of the buttons.

## Files
- `tools/v8-src/03-fighter.js`, `14-valor-boxer.js`, `32-infernoth-brawler.js`. `sw.js`: cache `titan-clash-v8.4.3`.

## Tested
VALOR and INFERNOTH with a Titan Core: both fired their real ultimate with the cutscene, low-health ultimate still unused. Phone 844x390 screenshot: left and right HUD clear. No errors.
