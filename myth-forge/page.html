How to build in Mythic Forge

1. Start

  • Skeleton tab → Starters: Hero Mech, Basalt Kaiju or Sailback Spinosaur (turtle shells and parts are in the Turtle Kit category). Pull them apart and remix.
  • Or pick a Body type. Every body type comes with a smooth base body (like a mannequin), so you always have a whole creature to work on.
  • Body shape sliders (Skeleton tab): height, bulk, arm/leg/neck/tail length, head size. Every part refits as you slide, like a game character creator.

Swapping parts

  • Pick a part, then tap a body piece (or its bone). It swaps in for what was there and sizes itself to fit: torsos fill the chest to the hips, heads sit on the neck, arms and legs fill the limb, hands and jaws match.
  • Fix anything after in the Move tab. ↻ Fit to body snaps a part back to its fitted size. ↻ Fit all parts (Skeleton tab) does every part.
  • 🧱 Base body on/off hides or brings back the mannequin pieces. Your own parts stay.

2. Add parts

  1. Add tab → pick a category, then tap a part.
  2. Tap an orange dot (a bone) or the body. Limbs, tails, necks and wings line up with the bone. Small parts stick on the surface.
  3. Mirror L/R puts a copy on the other side. Weapons go into the palm of a hand.
  4. Double-tap a part in the grid to add it right away: on the bone you picked, or the first bone that fits it (heads on the head, hands on the hands…).

3. Move it

  • Move tab: tap a part, then drag the arrows (Move), rings (Turn) or boxes (Stretch). The Size slider makes it bigger or smaller.
  • 🧲 Sticky: drag any part with your finger or pen and it sticks where you let go, like a toy. Drop it off the model and it goes in the 📦 Parts box (top of the Add tab) for later.
  • 🫳 Mold (on by default): a part you drop squishes around what it lands on, like pressing clay together. Sculpt › Undo all sculpt un-squishes it.
  • 🧩 Break apart: turns a row of spikes or crystals into single pieces you can move one by one.

4. Look closer

  • 🔍 Focus shows only that part, big. 🪟 Focus window keeps a small zoomed window on the part while you see the whole creature (drag in it to spin, pinch to zoom, Size S/M/L).
  • One finger turns the view, two fingers pinch and move. Double-tap centers there. FX / View tab → Arrow pad if you want the buttons.

5. Shape, sculpt and paint

  • Shape tab: sliders for "maker" parts. Sculpt tab: push and pull like clay (S Pen sculpts, fingers turn the view). Paint tab: tap a material, or 🖌 brush paint. 🎯 One piece only: tap one spike / crystal / bit on the model, then a material, and only that piece changes.

6. Bone numbers

  • Every bone has a number. Under More: # All, # Picked (only the bone you're on plus the numbers you type, like 2, 28) or # Off, and Fade.

7. Save and use it

  • ⋯ → Save as… keeps a named copy. Loading a starter replaces what's on screen.
  • ⋯ → Download model (.glb) to put it in the game.

Tip: the "More ▾" under each tab has the extra buttons.
