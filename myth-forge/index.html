How to build in Mythic Forge

🏁 Start

  • Pick a starter to remix, or an empty body type. Every body type comes with a smooth base body (like a mannequin).
  • Body shape sliders: height, bulk, arm/leg/neck/tail length, head size. Every part refits as you slide.

🧩 Build › ＋ Add parts

  1. Pick a category, then press and hold a part and drag it onto the model. (Tap it, then tap the model also works. Double-tap adds it fast.)
  2. 🦴 Snap to bone: limbs, tails, necks, wings and heads line up with the bone and size to fit. Main parts swap out what was there.
  3. ✋ Free: the part goes exactly where you let go, on the body or floating in the air.
  4. 🪞 Both sides puts a mirrored copy on the other side. Weapons go into the palm.

🧩 Build › ✋ Move & size

  • Tap a part to pick it (orange outline), then drag it. Let go on the body: it sticks there. Let go in the air: it floats there (and still moves with the closest bone). Let go on the 📦 box at the top: it goes in the Parts box for later.
  • Touching anything that isn't picked turns the view, so you won't knock parts off by accident.
  • 🎯 Arrows for fine-tuning: Move / Turn / Stretch handles. The Size slider makes it bigger or smaller.
  • 📄 Copy / 📋 Paste works between creatures, saves and the Workshop.
  • More: 🧩 Break apart (a row of spikes, or a part you saved, into single pieces), 🫳 Mold, Flip, Hide, Solo.

🛠 Workshop: build a part by itself

  • Build tab or Start tab › 🛠 Workshop. No skeleton: stack shapes and parts, sculpt, paint. A green box shows the normal size.
  • 💾 Save part (top bar) puts it in My Parts, then drag it onto any creature. ↩ Creature goes back.
  • To change a saved part later: add it, 🧩 Break apart, edit the pieces.

🎨 Paint · 🗿 Sculpt · ⚙

  • Paint: tap a part, then a skin. Brushes below. ✨ Effects has Fade away, Drip and Glitter fall.
  • Sculpt: push and pull like clay (S Pen sculpts, fingers turn the view). 🎚 Shape sliders for maker parts.
  • ⚙: 🦴 Skeleton & pose (bones, poses, make your own skeleton, animations) and 👁 View & look (camera, game look, background).

🔍 Focus

  • Top right: 🔍 Focus shows only the picked part, big. 🪟 Window keeps a zoomed window on it.

Save and use it

  • ⋯ → Save as… keeps a named copy. ⋯ → Download model (.glb) to put it in the game.
